## english: what the bot has learned

**Keep**

- Lead with a concrete, relatable scenario or question tied to a real task, e.g. 'Can one selfie become an ID photo?'. This is the only non-manual reel in the top three, at 446 views and 396 reach.
- Show a before/after on one task (selfie to ID photo). It had the highest reach and views of any reel, but only 6.2s average watch and 1 save, so it earns attention without holding it.
- Point to the website or tool directly. The two top-scoring reels (#ilovedocuments and 'check our website too') got shares (18 and 0) and saves (0 and 1). Treat that as a weak signal: they are manual posts with tiny samples, and one reel drives most of the shares.
- Keep the hook short and plain. The best reels have simple hooks, and watch time tops out around 8–9s, so put the payoff in the first ~8s.
- Only the selfie and ID-photo reel is a clean, repeatable template. The others are manual or one-offs, so don't generalise further from 8 posts.

**Avoid**

- Story-led hooks with a named character ('Why did Rohan miss his own interview?', 'Priya had 400 rows to type by tonight.'). They scored 114 and 81 with 0–1 comments and no saves or shares. Rohan held viewers (8.2s) but Priya did not (4.8s), so the format is not proven bad, only unproven.
- Generic cheat-sheet or listicle hooks ('Stop paying the cyber cafe for these 5 things'). It had the lowest watch time of the group (4.1s) and no shares, though it did get 2 saves. That is a weak sign the content is useful but the hook does not hold people.
- Plain how-to questions with no emotional stakes ('How do you send 4 photos as one PDF?'). They got 0 interactions across 140 views, even though viewers watched 7.0s.
- Vague 'any job, one website' promises ('Any PDF or photo job – one free website'). It had 1.7s average watch, so viewers dropped almost immediately. It was also very new (1 view in the last hour), so the number may still change.
- Long 24–32s scripted reels that don't convert. All the scripted reels except the selfie one scored under 120 with almost no shares. Length is confounded with hook and topic, so test it before cutting.

**Next Tests**

- Rerun the before/after format on a different task (e.g. compress a photo to 50KB or merge PDFs) with an outcome-first question hook. Compare views, reach and watch time against the selfie reel's 446 views and 6.2s.
- Same selfie/ID-photo topic and hook, but cut to about 10–12s, to see whether a shorter reel lifts average watch above 6.2s and brings saves or shares.
- Add an explicit save or share prompt ('send this to someone who needs it') to the reel that works best. Shares were 18 on one manual post and 0 on almost every scripted one, so see whether a prompt moves that.

## hinglish: what the bot has learned

**Keep**

- Hooks that ask for a specific, familiar Indian document or password (Aadhaar PDF password, 'PDF ko edit kaise karein?') in the question/secret_answer format. The top 3 by score use this or a close variant, with 1.2k–2k views and the highest saves (24 and 12) and shares (18 on the top reel).
- Pick topics people already search for and need solved (unlock Aadhaar, compress PDF, PDF to Word). These reached 1.3k–2k views, while the niche or generic tasks (image_to_pdf, pdf_to_excel) got about 120.
- Write the hook in Hinglish and name the exact pain point or document. 'Aadhaar PDF ka password...' got 1966 views and 18 shares. The English 'What if your PDF is too big?' also worked (1322 views), so a concrete pain point may matter more than the language.
- Open with the hook in the first second and deliver the answer fast. Reels with avg_watch around 8–9s, like the top reel at 8.2s and compress_pdf at 8.8s, led the ranking. This is a correlation from a small sample.
- Make the reel something people save or send to others. The top reel has 24 saves and 18 shares. The practical 'password/how-to' angle seems to prompt that, but with 11 reels this is still a hypothesis.

**Avoid**

- Generic 'how to do X' hooks about low-urgency tasks (image_to_pdf, pdf_to_excel). They got about 116–123 views, avg_watch of 3.4–3.5s, and almost no engagement.
- Hooks with no tie to the topic. 'Masked Aadhaar kya hota hai?' (199 views, 4.6s watch, 0 saves) did poorly. The story_open 'Egypt ke tomb mein shahad' (221 views) and the 'Charging 100% tak mat karo' hook (435 views, 6.8s watch) were also weak, and the charging hook has nothing to do with its resize_image_kb topic.
- Hooks that don't promise a specific outcome, like the bold_claim 'Most people think a PDF page can't become a photo' (187 views, 0 saves, 0 shares). A claim with no payoff got no saves or shares.
- Posting the same topic again with a weaker hook. unlock_pdf was posted twice with e-PAN and Egypt hooks (274 and 221 views), against 1966 views for the Aadhaar version. Hook specificity seems to matter more than topic alone.
- Broad catch-all hooks. 'PDF ya photo ka koi bhi kaam ho – ek hi website kaafi hai' got 919 views. That's decent, but it has few likes, comments and saves relative to the Aadhaar and PDF-edit hooks, and it doesn't name a single pain point.

**Next Tests**

- Run the same 'X ka password kya hota hai?' format on other widely used documents (PAN card, bank statement, payslip, Form 16) to see if the Aadhaar result repeats or was a one-off. Keep the format and length constant and change only the document.
- Test the same topic (compress_pdf_kb) in Hinglish against the English 'What if...' hook. This separates language from topic, since the English hook did well.
- Retest the weak topics (pdf_to_excel, image_to_pdf) with a specific pain-point hook in the Aadhaar style, such as 'Bank statement PDF ka table Excel mein kaise layein?'. This shows whether the topic or the hook caused the low views. Also compare avg_watch_s against the 8–9s of the top reels.
